OrbitSchool Privacy Overview

This overview describes the OrbitSchool single-app classroom features. The school hosting OrbitSchool should review and approve the final privacy notice before use with real students.

Email sign-in

When a person creates an account or signs in, OrbitSchool uses their email address to send a one-time verification code. The app does not ask users to create an OrbitSchool password. Email addresses are not shown to other classroom participants.

Classroom sharing

When a student joins a class, the teacher can see the student's chosen display name and learning activity shared by OrbitSchool, such as XP, quizzes completed, streak and current app section. Teachers do not see the student's email address through the class roster.

Camera, microphone and screen sharing

Camera, microphone and screen sharing are optional browser permissions used only inside a live classroom. Participants can turn their camera or microphone off, stop sharing their screen or leave the room. This build does not provide cloud recording.

Guest access

Guests may join a class with a display name, where the school enables guest access. Their class participation is not connected to a registered account.

School responsibility

The school must decide what information is necessary, obtain required approvals and notices, select a suitable hosting and email provider, restrict access to the stored app data and define an appropriate retention period before real student use.
