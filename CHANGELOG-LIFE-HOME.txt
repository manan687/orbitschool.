ORBITSCHOOL — LIFE & HOME UPGRADE
================================

This additive update expands the character, pet, room and local item economy while preserving the existing OrbitSchool study tools and AI integration.

CHARACTER STUDIO
----------------
- Added boy and girl character style options.
- Expanded skin-tone choices.
- Added coloured shirts, pants, joggers, shorts and a skirt option.
- Expanded hairstyles and hair colours.
- Added headwear, glasses/accessories, shoes and facial expressions.
- Added outfit randomisation, saved looks and emote actions.
- Added a 3D character preview using Three.js when available, with a canvas-drawn animated preview fallback.

PET SANCTUARY
-------------
- Expanded the companion catalogue to eight pets: Comet Pup, Nebula Kitten, Cloud Bunny, Ember Fox, Nova Dragon, Bamboo Panda, Frost Penguin and Ember Red Panda.
- Added pet naming, a bond counter, care statistics, feeding, play, petting and grooming actions.
- Added pet accessories and snack purchases.
- Added individual 3D pet details and idle motion when Three.js is available, with an animated canvas fallback.
- Pet care is optional and does not affect study streaks. Pet actions do not award study XP or coins.

3D ROOM
-------
- Expanded the room with a study corner, bed, rugs, bookshelf, plant, pet bed, aquarium, projector, trophy shelf, window, guitar, beanbag, wall art, gaming chair, ceiling stars, planet mobile and window seat.
- Added six wall themes and four floor themes, with level-gated coin purchases.
- Added a live 3D room scene with drag-to-orbit and scroll-to-zoom controls when Three.js is available.
- Added avatar and pet presence, room lighting control, furniture placement/removal and a canvas fallback.

ECONOMY
-------
- Uses the app's existing XP, level and Orbit Coins values.
- Cosmetic items, companions, furniture and room themes have coin prices and level requirements.
- Study tools remain free; XP determines level and is not spent.
- Purchases are stored locally in this browser build. Server-backed ownership and anti-tamper validation are not included.

TECHNICAL NOTES
---------------
- New functionality is in orbit-life.js and orbit-life.css.
- Existing study, AI, planner, flashcard, game and school modules are retained.
- The service-worker cache version is updated to include the new files.
- Three.js is loaded by the existing app from its external CDN. The 3D previews require that library and WebGL; animated canvas previews are used if 3D cannot initialise.
- The build is a browser/PWA project, not a signed Android App Bundle.
- Full interactive testing on physical Chromebook and Android devices remains to be completed.
